# Hardware Requirements

## Minimum Requirements

### CPU
- **Architecture**: x86_64 or ARM64
- **Cores**: 2+ physical cores
- **RAM**: 4 GB minimum (8 GB+ recommended)

### Storage
- **Repository**: ~100 MB
- **Dependencies**: ~500 MB

## Recommended Configurations

### Development
- **CPU**: 4+ cores
- **RAM**: 16 GB
- **Storage**: SSD 100 GB+

### Production
- **CPU**: 8+ cores
- **RAM**: 32 GB+
- **Storage**: NVMe SSD 500 GB+
